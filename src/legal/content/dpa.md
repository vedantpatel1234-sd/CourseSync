# Data Processing Addendum

This addendum forms part of the agreement between **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("**Provider**") and the subscribing organization ("**Customer**") for CourseSync. It applies to personal information that Provider handles for Customer. If it conflicts with the main agreement on the handling of personal information, this addendum controls.

## 1. Roles

Customer decides what personal information is entered into CourseSync and why. Provider handles it only on Customer's behalf, as a service provider. Customer remains responsible for having the authority to collect and use the information, and for any notices and consents its own law requires.

## 2. Scope

The details of the processing are in the Schedule below. **CourseSync is not intended to hold student information**, and Customer agrees not to enter it.

## 3. Provider's commitments

Provider will:

1. handle personal information only to provide, secure and support CourseSync and as Customer documents in writing, unless the law requires otherwise (in which case Provider will tell Customer first where it lawfully can);
2. make sure everyone with access is bound by confidentiality and given access only as needed;
3. keep the information separated from other customers' information and protected by the measures in the [Security overview](/legal/security), and not materially reduce that protection during the agreement;
4. use only the subprocessors listed at [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors), bind them to equivalent obligations, give Customer **[30] days'** notice of changes, and remain responsible for them;
5. not sell the information, use it for advertising, or combine it with other customers' information except as needed to run the service.

## 4. Assistance

Provider will, on request and within reasonable time, help Customer to: respond to access and correction requests from individuals; complete a privacy impact assessment (including describing data flows, locations and subprocessors); and respond to a regulator's inquiry about the service.

## 5. Incidents

If Provider becomes aware of unauthorized access to, or loss or disclosure of, Customer's personal information, it will notify Customer without undue delay and within **[72 hours]**, describe what is known, take reasonable steps to contain it, and cooperate with Customer's investigation and any required notifications.

## 6. Location and transfers

The primary database is hosted in **Canada** **[VERIFY]**. Some subprocessors may handle information outside Canada, as shown on the Subprocessors page. Customer is responsible for deciding whether that is acceptable under its own legal obligations, and Provider will give Customer the information it needs to decide. AI features can be turned off for Customer's organization, which prevents information being sent to the AI provider.

## 7. Return and deletion

During the agreement, Customer can ask for an export of its information. When the agreement ends Provider will, on request, return a complete export (CSV files, including a plain-language schedule) and then delete the organization's data and every user's sign-in account within **[30] days**. Provider will not delete an organization's data until the export has been produced and checked against the live records, unless Customer instructs otherwise in writing. Copies in backups are deleted on the backup provider's normal schedule **[VERIFY retention]**. Provider will confirm deletion in writing on request.

On Customer's request Provider will also delete an individual's records during the agreement, replacing that person's name and email in activity records and weekly summaries as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 8. Information and audits

On reasonable written request, and no more than **[once a year]** unless there has been an incident, Provider will provide information needed to show it is meeting this addendum, such as answers to a security questionnaire. On-site audits are by agreement.

## 9. Liability

Liability under this addendum is subject to the limits in the main agreement. **[LAWYER TO CONFIRM.]**
