# Privacy Policy

**Effective:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains what personal information CourseSync handles, why, who else sees it, and the choices people have. [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("**we**") provides CourseSync to colleges and universities (our "**Customers**"). **In almost all cases we handle personal information on behalf of the Customer, which decides what is entered and why.** Questions about a specific school's use of the information are best directed to that school's privacy office; we will help them respond.

## What we handle

| Information | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, work email, role (administrator, coordinator, instructor) | Entered by the Customer's administrators, or by you when you accept an invitation | To create and secure accounts and show who is assigned to what |
| Department, title, maximum teaching hours | Entered by the Customer | To plan workloads |
| Availability, section preferences, course qualifications | Entered by the instructor or the Customer | To match instructors to sections |
| Course, section, term and assignment records | Entered by the Customer | The scheduling service itself |
| Résumé content (if an administrator uses résumé reading) | A PDF the administrator uploads | To pre-fill an instructor's details. The file is sent to our AI provider to be read and is **not stored by CourseSync** **[VERIFY]** |
| Notifications, weekly summaries, draft schedules | Generated by the service | Features of the service |
| Activity records (who did what, and when) | Generated by the service | Security and accountability for administrators |
| Acceptance of these terms (who, when, which version) | Recorded when you accept | To keep a record of consent |
| AI usage (organization, user, model, amount of text processed; not the content) | Generated by the service | To apply plan limits and operate the service |
| Technical data such as IP address and browser type | Sent automatically when you use the site | To deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service |

CourseSync is not designed to hold information about students, and we ask Customers not to enter it.

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use analytics or advertising trackers. We do not use Customer content to build marketing profiles.

## Cookies and similar technology

CourseSync stores a sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in. It has no advertising or analytics cookies. The site loads a typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads **[consider self-hosting the font]**.

## How long we keep information

While the Customer's agreement is active we keep information so the service works. After it ends, we give the Customer a complete export on request (spreadsheet-format files) and then delete everything the organization held, including every user's sign-in account, within **[30] days**, except copies in backups, which expire on the provider's normal schedule **[VERIFY]**.

If a single person's account is deleted during the agreement, their records (availability, preferences, qualifications, assignments and notifications) are deleted with it, and their name and email are replaced in activity records and weekly summaries wherever we can find them. Generated text can refer to someone in ways we cannot always match (for example by first name only), and the organization's administrators can ask us to review a specific record. Activity records are otherwise kept for the life of the agreement so administrators can review them.
